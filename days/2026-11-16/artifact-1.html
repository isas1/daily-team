Clay Mend

Shape the clay, then undo. Gold marks what came back.

Demo

Drag to press clay

Demo terrain. Arrow keys move the brush, Shift moves faster, Enter applies the tool.

Tool
Keys
  • Arrows move the brush. Shift moves faster.
  • Enter or Space applies the tool.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes. Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. Cmd on macOS.
  • Redo reruns the tool from its seed, so the same pebbles return.
