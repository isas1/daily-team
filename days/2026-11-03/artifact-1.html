Zero Hour Rehearsal

Type one launch time. Press each cue from T-7d to T+1d. Read the countdown in light and dark for viewers in five zones. Copy a snippet that carries every state.

Sample launch: Fieldnote 2.0, a made-up product, 10 Nov 2026 16:00 UTC. Local sample data only. Nothing here connects to a live service, an account or the URL you enter.
