Handshake Bench

Two toy handshake machines and one wire. You deliver, drop, duplicate or delay each packet. The clock moves only when you press.

Local demo · sample toy protocol · no network

Step log

    Keys

    D Deliver · X Drop · U Duplicate · L Delay · T Clock +1 s · J Jump to next timer · R Retry after a failure. Packet keys act on the packet with the dark left edge, or on the packet that holds focus.

    Toy protocol

    • Initiator sends HELLO with a counter. With no matching REPLY in 5 s, it resends with the next counter. It gives up after 3 tries.
    • Responder answers a fresh HELLO with REPLY and waits 8 s for CONFIRM. It closes the session on any replayed counter.
    • The protocol is a pure function, handle(state, event), fed by your presses. Every step keeps a snapshot, so any step can be retried as a new run.
