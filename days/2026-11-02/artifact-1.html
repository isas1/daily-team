        • Flight deck designer (lead): the fixed 96 px label slot, the state word kept on the 44 px face, the same 44 px height in every state, and the plain words Preparing, Waiting, Ready.
        • Cartographic designer: the 320 px frame board, the 44 px height measured after each word swap, and the 2 px focus outline drawn outside the repeat tap.
