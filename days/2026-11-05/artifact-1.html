Shelf Read

Read a curated link list one shelf of 8 entries at a time. Mark each entry Keep, Stale or Move. The rail records which shelves are read, and your place is kept for next time. Export a pruned markdown file with a list of changes.

Sample list · local and fictional · no link is fetched

    Shelf

      Tally

      Keys

      • J K next or previous entry (arrows inside the shelf)
      • 1 Keep · 2 Stale · 3 Move
      • [ ] previous or next shelf
      • U undo last mark (up to 50)

      Pressing the same mark again clears it. After each mark, focus rests on the first unmarked entry of the shelf.

      Export

      Stale entries go to ## Archive at the end of the file.
