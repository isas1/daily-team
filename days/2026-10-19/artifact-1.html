Field notes, bench 4

Read.The line keeps it.

Scroll. The rim around the window fills. Stop for three seconds and a pin goes in. Press the rim anywhere to go there.

Before

You came from somewhere. A link, a search, a name someone said. You arrived carrying it. The line starts at the top left, where you came in.

I

The form

A mount starts with a form. Wood, wire, wrapped tow. The skin goes over it later.

Reading works the same way. The page is the form. What you take from it is the skin.

II

Setting

Hide dries hard in about a week. After that it holds its shape for a century.

The line behind your reading head cools the same way. Near white where you are. Amber two seconds later. Grey after twenty. Read slowly and it stays warm longer.

III

Pins

When the skin is wet you pin it to hold an expression. One pin at the eye. One at the lip.

Stop reading for three seconds and the rim takes a pin. It lands exactly where you stopped, even in the middle of a sentence like this one, so the next visit starts mid-thought.

IV

Labels

A specimen without a label is a curiosity. Hover or focus a pin and it says where and when. Press it and you are back there.

V

Keeping

Close the tab. Come back next week. The pins are still there, gone grey. The part you read is still marked, faint, under the new line.

Nothing here asks a server. It lives in this browser, up to twelve pins per page.

VI

Hands

Tab to the rim. Left and Right step between pins. Enter on a pin goes there. Enter on the head sets a pin by hand. Home and End go to the start and the finish. Delete pulls a pin.

VII

How it is measured

rim L = 0 px
r = 0 ÷ 0 = 0.000
drawn = L × r = 0 px
0 s 2 s 20 s

r is scrollTop over the scrollable height. Each 1% of the rim keeps the moment the head passed it, and its colour comes from the seconds since. Cost: one SVG, two hundred short paths, one timer. That fits the budget.

After

You leave with something, half read or read through. When the rim is full it parts at the lower right. That gap is the way out.

Specimen: this page. Read through 0 times.

Taxidermist, lead: pins that last across visits, the faint mark of what you read, the before and after labels. Wheelwright: the line runs the whole rim, and pins land mid-sentence. Glassblower: the hot head, cooling by time, 12 px corners, the 24 px band. Daedalus: every point answers a press, and the door at the end.
