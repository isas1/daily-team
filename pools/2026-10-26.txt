#@ tasks
Accordion in bellows: a section draws breath as it opens, and the last one breathes out.
Slider in pendulum: a weighted bob swings to the value and slowly comes to rest.
Toast in smoke: a notice rises, holds its shape long enough to read, and drifts apart.
Stepper in split flap: each count clatters through the flaps and settles on the new number.
Rating in thermal: a press leaves a warm print that cools to the score that was given.
Search field in a sieve: letters shake a mesh, and only the matching words stay on top.
Date picker in tide: drag the tide line along a shore of days, and it leaves one day wet.
Progress ring in phyllotaxis: seeds spiral out from the center until the head is full.
Pagination in flipbook: pages riffle under the thumb and stop with the number in view.
Breadcrumb in footprints: the trail back up is a line of prints that deepen as each is reached.
Copy button in carbon paper: one tap presses a copy through, and the second sheet shows it worked.
Password field in crystal growth: each character feeds a crystal that grows larger and more ordered.
Tag input in beads: each word becomes a bead on a thread, and a tap slips one off the end.
Color swatch in dye bath: dip the cloth, and it slowly takes one color from a small set of vats.
Skeleton in graphite rubbing: the shape of the content appears as if rubbed over a hidden relief.
Empty state in tide pool: an empty list is a still pool with one small creature waiting.
Countdown in marbled ink: rings of ink spread on water and close in as the moment nears.
Segmented control in iron filings: a magnet slides between three choices and the filings follow.
Checkbox in embroidery: a stitch crosses the box, can be unpicked, or stops halfway as a half state.
Reorder list in rope: items hang on a rope, and lifting one moves the slack along the line.
Initials stack in shadow play: a few figures overlap behind a screen, and the rest are a count.
Badge in droplets: the count grows as drops merge, and a drop splits off when it goes down.
Sparkline in seismograph: a needle draws the numbers inside a sentence and settles at rest.
Undo in rewind: a mistake runs back along its own path, the way tape plays in reverse.
Key hint in ripples: each key on screen sends one ring across still water when it is pressed.
Character counter in sand: grains drain as text is typed, showing how much room is left.
Theme switch in eclipse: light and dark trade places as one disc slides across another.
Dial in cymatics: turning changes the tone, and sand gathers on the nodal lines when it locks.
File drop in a well: a dropped file falls, rings spread, and the echo confirms it arrived.
Scroll progress in a wick: a thin flame burns along the edge as the page is read.
Split view in surface tension: two pools share one edge that the hand can drag.
Carousel in a zoetrope: cards spin past a slit and stop one at a time, by swipe or key.
Status dot in fireflies: one light pulses slowly when away, steadily when online, and fast when busy.
Command menu in magnetism: typing pulls the matching actions up out of a scattered field.
+ Inline edit in hot wax: a line of text softens into a field when clicked and sets hard again when left.
+ Code input in dominoes: each digit drops into its box and tips the next box ready for the one after.
+ Signature pad in wet ink: a mark drawn by hand bleeds and dries, and one wipe lifts it away to start again.
+ Timeline in tree rings: a few dated moments grow outward as rings, and today is the newest, softest edge.
+ Time picker in shadow: an hour and a minute are set by moving the light until the shadow falls on them.
+ Level meter in boiling water: bubbles rise faster as the sound gets louder and settle as it fades.
