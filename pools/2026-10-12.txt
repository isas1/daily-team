Under 30 KB in total.

Lowercase only.

Uses sound as well as sight.

#@ tasks
Tide: something that arrives, stays a while, and leaves.
Night shift: what happens while everyone else sleeps.
Lost property: a thing someone left behind and the story it carries.
Weather inside: a mood that behaves like the sky.
Small world: a place too little to notice until you look closely.
Echo: a sound that comes back changed.
Map of nowhere: a place that does not exist but can be explored.
Hand-me-down: something passed between generations that wears as it goes.
Threshold: the moment between outside and inside.
Slow time: something that takes a hundred years, watched in a minute.
Swarm: many small things that decide together.
Shadow: what an object becomes when the light moves.
Recipe for a feeling: ingredients, steps, and what comes out of the oven.
Cabinet: things that belong together for a reason only the keeper knows.
Ruin: what is left after something grand falls, and what grows there.
Signal: a message sent to someone who may never receive it.
Hunger: something that eats, grows, and must be fed.
Seasons: one thing seen through a whole year.
Knot: something tangled that can be undone, or made worse.
Beacon: a light that guides someone home.
Mask: a face to wear and what it changes.
Underground: what lives below the floor of the visible world.
First word: the moment something learns to speak.
Crossing: a long migration made by many at once.
Garden: something planted today that only shows itself later.
Fortune: a reading of what might come next.
Wrong mirror: something that shows the visitor back to themselves, but changed.
Bestiary: a creature that has never been recorded.
Ritual: a small act repeated until it means something.
Erosion: something worn away a little each time it is touched.
Constellation: points joined into a picture someone can name.
Pulse: a rhythm that keeps something alive.
Fog: something only half seen until the visitor moves closer.
Time capsule: words sealed now and opened later.
Nest: a home built from scraps.
Fire: something that warms, spreads, and leaves ash.
Instrument: something that makes a sound only the visitor can play.
Rumour: a story that changes each time it is passed on.
Wake: the trail left by something that has already gone.
Hidden room: a space that opens only to someone who looks closely.
